THE PROJECT

Automated Dual Fan Circuit

An Arduino-based system designed to respond to temperature and light fluctuations using two DC motor fans.

PROJECT GOAL

The challenge

We set out to address increased air temperature with an automated fan circuit, a practical problem that can affect people throughout the day. The project requirements included using at least two sensors and two motors. An LCD display was an additional stretch goal.
