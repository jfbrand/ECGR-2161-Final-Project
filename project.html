THE PROJECT

Automated Dual Fan Circuit

Utilizing the Arduino board to automatically respond to temperature and light fluctuations with two DC motor fans

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem

We set out to address the problem of increased air temperature with an automated fan circuit. This is a practical problem that almost everyone can face throughout the day.

The circuit was intended to fulfill the minimum requirements of using at least two sensors and two motors to accomplish our goal. Out stretch goal was to utilized the LCD display to show the temperature read by the sensor.
